03 / Your next rabbit hole

NO SUBSCRIPTION. NO ROYALTIES.

A room. A script.
A possibility.

Ordinary GDScript. Your own assets. A small framework you can actually read.

Clone the project, open it in Godot 4.7.2 and press F5. The sample is your starting point: swap in your cast, draw your rooms and write what happens next.

Read the getting-started guide ↗

Verified with Godot 4.7.2. Other versions haven’t been verified yet.

START HERE
# One repo. One little adventure.
git clone https://github.com/ajokela/cuebox.git
cd cuebox
godot --editor --path .
rooms / radio_shack.gdGDSCRIPT
extends "res://engine/room_script.gd"

func use_radio(who: String, item: String) -> void:
    if item == "fuse":
        take(who, item)
        set_flag("radio_ready")
        await say(who, "WE’RE BACK ON THE AIR.")
    else:
        await say(who, "IT NEEDS A NEW FUSE.")

↳ You write the interesting part.

Small framework. Clear boundaries.

Made to be understood.
And made your own.

Cuebox is a code-first framework on top of Godot. You author the content; it runs the adventure. It isn’t a visual puzzle editor or an art generator.

The runtime gives you local saves and extension points. Your game owns its content pipeline, save migrations and platform integrations.
