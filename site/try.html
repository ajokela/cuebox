Try Cuebox · nothing to install

Change the code.
Play your version.

This is The Last Signal, Cuebox’s two-room sample, as plain GDScript. Edit it, press Run, and the real engine plays your version right here. Your edits stay in this browser.

⌘/Ctrl+Enter runs · Tab indents · Esc, then Tab, leaves

Downloads the engine (about 10 MB) only when you press Run.

  1. Lines Rae says, rooms, flags and errors appear here. Type below to talk to the running game.
>

Say something else

In rooms/shack/shack.gd, change a line inside say(...) and press Run. Rae says yours.

Add an object

Add a hotspot to rooms/shack/geometry.gd, say {"id":"plant", "name":"PLANT", "rect":Rect2(200,70,30,40), "walk":Vector2(215,130), "face":"back", "prop":""}, then a func use_plant(who, item) in shack.gd.

Poke the running game

The console runs one line against the current room’s script: flag("fuse_taken"), pick_up("rae", "fuse"), use_radio("rae", "fuse"), game.state.flags.
